Questions

New to drone services?

Here are answers to the questions people usually ask first.

Do I need to know anything about drones?

Not at all. Just tell me what you want to show and how you plan to use it. I'll recommend the right shots and take care of everything else.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the location and what you need, but quotes are always free and up-front. Send a quick message and you'll get a clear price before anything is booked.

Is it legal and safe?

Yes. I'm an FAA Part 107 certified remote pilot. I check airspace and weather before every job and request any authorization the location requires.

Which areas do you serve?

Stafford, Fredericksburg, Spotsylvania and the surrounding Northern Virginia communities. If you're a bit farther out, just ask.

Do I need to be there during the flight?

Nope, though you're welcome to watch! I just need access to the area and a heads-up about anything I should know.

What if the weather is bad?

Safety comes first, so if it's too windy or rainy I'll reschedule at no extra cost.

Can drone photos replace a roof or property inspection?

No. Aerial photos are great visual documentation, but they don't replace an inspection by a licensed roofer, engineer or insurance adjuster. Many people share the photos with those professionals.

How quickly will I get my photos?

Usually within a few days. If you're on a deadline, let me know and I'll do my best to make it work.
